## Getting Started

### How free-to-play friendly is Dragon Village 3, including Story and PvP?

The game is extremely f2p friendly. There are some events where paying players of course have better chances to get better items, but that doesn't mean you have no chance in getting them. HB has a good understanding of where f2p players can land and what they can afford.

Story is very well paced. I got through the story in a few weeks, where some stages were surprisingly cruel to my roster. For PvP there's a high difference between Arena and Colosseum. Colosseum can be really easy for f2p to reach Gold and Platinum, while Arena is pretty much unbeatable in the beginning, though it gets better when you get better dragons.

### What should a new player focus on first?

Focus on getting as many dragons as possible. Don't put too much into a single dragon since the element matchup table can be your best friend - as well as it can be your worst enemy. Get as many diamonds as possible and don't spend them on the normal draw. Only draw if you get tickets in your first weeks.

### Which Dragons should a beginner build first, and which would you recommend for each element?

Start with one dragon of each element. Try to get access to Soul-element dragons as fast as possible, so you can breed every dragon quickly.

For the beginning of your adventure, Magnello is a great starting point that can even wield Magic Orbs very well.

For Wind, once you get Typhoon, he is your best Wind dragon for a long while until you unlock Plumos.

For Water, you have Frost Dragon which you get as your first Legendary in the beginning. Don't make the mistake assuming he would be bad because he is a low-star Legendary! He's often undervalued and makes an awesome tank!

Steel is weird. If you have Obex, use him! Even low rank he can deal reliable damage, but when you want a great Epic, there's basically only Sharp Tail I can recommend.

For Earth you want Egg Dragon. He's obtainable through the draw feature and you'll spend a lot of tickets to get him to a good rank. Trust me, he's worth it!

For Lightning there are two: one of them is Thunderbolt Dragon. He'll get important later for the Raid feature, but if you don't have or want him, there's Cloud Dragon as well from the Draw.

Light... Get Haze as fast as possible. Dilrang is nice as well, but you want Haze. You will also need him for the Raid.

For Dark it gets interesting. First pick is Serpent Dragon for the same reason like Haze and Thunderbolt. Lunera is a wonderful pick as well, if you want to build her with draws. But often overlooked there is another one in Lagtail. Make sure to get some Hope Gems on him.

Dream is incredibly difficult to find a good substitute for a dragon using Dream as secondary element. There is Alien... But he's lacking everything I want him to do. For some reason Alien Dragon just isn't making the cut in like any team I tried. If you got Magnas from the event, use him. He's better than Alien in every single way.

And finally Soul: Mummy Dragon will be your #1 pick here. Once you get Lucio and more copies for him, he'll slowly take Mummy Dragon's spot.

### Are low-star Legendaries worth using, or are well-built Epics better?

It REALLY depends. I get very high success rate with low-star Legendaries. You have to know how to build them correctly, then they are much better due to better and more individual abilities. They also do better in Duel/Solo PvP.

Flamber, Frost, Florea, even Power and Lightning can be great Dragons at lower Star count. At the same time, a well-built Epic can absolutely outperform a badly built Legendary.

Don't overdraw in the normal draws just to force Stars onto a Legendary. You will get them slowly but steadily.

### How should I strengthen a new Dragon, and what matters most?

First of all you need to get them to a good level. A level 30 isn't doing much against higher levels, even when they have cracked stats otherwise. Then give them the best Orbs you got and any Gems that fit them well.

Level, Rank, Stars, Gems and Orbs all matter. A great Dragon is nothing with bad Orbs. As well as a bad Dragon isn't as bad when you put effort into him with Gems and Orbs. Try to raise everything equally until you hit a roadblock, then focus on other areas first until you have enough stuff to get the other areas on par.

### How far should I level a Dragon, and is level 50 worth the Food cost?

Level 40 is a good threshold in the community. I'd even say 36 is enough for most Dragons for Story. When you want to look into PvP or Raids, get them to level 40 at least.

Level 50 isn't worth it. If you want a good upper level, take 46, then a Dragon is basically already very strong, so that it can dominate other Dragons easily. But level 40 is probably your go-to when leveling a Dragon without having dedicated plans for it.

## Dragon Rank, Stars, Promotion & Transcendence

### What are Dragon Rank/Stat Grades, Stars and Promotion?

Dragon Rank/Stat Grades is the rank a stat can have. It ranges from 1-7 when a Dragon hatches and can be increased with Promotion and Limit Break first, then with Transcendence later.

The Stars are your Promotion Rank. The more you have, the better, and this ranges from 0-5. Promotion increases the number of Stars a Dragon has, adding a bonus based on how many Stars the Dragon has.

Once you get 5 Stars, Limit Break is unlocked and replaces Promotion.

### How do I get a Dragon to 7.0 Rank, and roughly how many duplicates can it take?

You need to Limit Break after you get to 5 Stars. Each Limit Break increases your Stat Grade differently and you can also use duplicates with higher Stat Grades to increase this even more.

Promotion alone usually isn't enough. You need Limit Breaks in like 99% of cases. Unless you got only Dragons with a 7 in one or even more stats in different stats each, there's no way you can get to 7.0 without Limit Breaks later.

The amount of copies is highly dependent on what you get for their stats. You need at least 6 Dragons for 5 Stars. Let's say you get to 5.0 with Promotion only: you will need at least like 7 more when you're lucky, but when you're unlucky you will need 20.

### What is the difference between a duplicate Dragon and a Promotion Stone, and can Promotion Stones be used for Limit Break?

Technically there is no difference for the Promotion itself. But higher Stat Grades from duplicate Dragons get transferred to the original Dragon, which Promotion Stones don't do.

Promotion Stones cannot be used for Limit Break. There are different Stones for different systems.
